## Minimum Garmin identity fixture

Provide one **actual staging/sandbox** publication and the corresponding completed activity, as sanitised JSON or original field excerpts. Keep the provider field names and types, including nested path, and list the originating endpoint and capture timing. Retain only:

- The MILE synthetic session ID or consistent alias, workout revision, scheduled local date, and the publication operation or receipt status.
- The exact Garmin workout identifier returned and verified by the publisher, the exact Garmin schedule identifier returned and verified by readback, and the matching receipt revision. Consistent aliases must preserve type and equality; annotate any hashing.
- The completed activity’s actual activity ID, the completion payload fields that refer to workout or schedule identity **if present**, and the relevant step references only if returned by Garmin. Include whether this came through a webhook, details endpoint or FIT file. Mark missing fields as absent.
- If available, a duplicate delivery of the same receipt or completion and one prior-revision record for comparison. A separate unrelated activity provides a useful negative control.

Remove credentials, device serials, real dates or identifiers that can identify athletes, route coordinates, unrelated records and health details. Preserve relative ordering and equality. Do not substitute a guessed field for absent provider identity. A matching date, distance or step index is insufficient for automatic linking. Keep live publishing, republication and automatic matching outside this rollout until the real contract is verified.
