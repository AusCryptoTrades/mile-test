# Batch 90 cycling development beta

Cycling uses the five-stage Build flow, the authenticated training document, the shared step validator and Calendar. The sport adapter is separate from Running coaching rules. Stored repeats and steps drive the review, Calendar and the local FIT adapter. Text editing invalidates the structured presentation until the text again matches the stored prescription. Completed sessions are excluded from cycling week regeneration.

## Evidence and decisions

| Topic | Implementation and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Progression and endurance | Start from confirmed sustainable time, availability and recent longest ride. Unconfirmed capacity, interruption and poor recovery use easy rides. Progression only follows a feasible planned week, with up to 12 extra minutes and a 6% ceiling per eligible week. This is a conservative coaching heuristic based on planned workload. It does not infer recovery or training tolerance. [ACSM position stand](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21694556/) |
| Specificity and intervals | Event type and preparation window select endurance, terrain and steady or variable effort recipes. Short high-intensity efforts use repeatable power or RPE and avoid chasing HR. Ride time is the primary prescription. The evidence supports different interval durations and intensities as tools. It does not validate this exact recipe schedule. [Seiler et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21812820/) |
| Distribution and fitness–fatigue | Most minutes are low intensity, with up to one demanding ride per week in the current adapter and fewer around other load. There is no fixed “80/20” prescription and no readiness inference from TSS. [Stöggl and Sperlich trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24550842/) · [Limits of the Banister model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1974899/) |
| Recovery and taper | Relative to planned weekly load, recovery weeks use a 72% cap, peak uses 85%, and an event taper uses 58%. These are reviewable defaults. The taper removes demanding interval sessions in this beta. It is a conservative implementation choice that differs from protocols that maintain intensity. [Bosquet et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17762369/) · [Endurance taper review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37163550/) |
| Concurrent training | Scheduling considers dates, known committed minutes, hard sessions and lower-body work. Unknown duration is treated conservatively. Keep sport-specific load metrics separate. Cycling holds progression when another sport has priority or known other minutes exceed cycling load. No integrated brick or triathlon plan is generated. [Schumann et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34757594/) |
| Power profiling and FTP | Report best observed contiguous 5 s, 1 min, 5 min and 20 min averages. They describe the recording rather than a maximal power curve. Retain dated FTP observations with method, source and confidence. Power prescriptions require a power meter or smart trainer, a recent valid FTP and sufficient confidence. The optional staged benchmark is sustained and non-maximal. It needs established training, normal recovery, power equipment, explicit opt-in and enough time. The app never multiplies a single effort by 95% or changes FTP automatically. [Power-duration profiling review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34708276/) · [Borszcz et al. validity study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29801189/) |

### Calculation conventions

- The seven cycling power zones have relative upper bounds of 55%, 75%, 90%, 105%, 120% and 150% of FTP, followed by the open upper zone. For integer watts, round each upper bound down and add one watt to start the next zone. Sweet spot is a coaching band at 88–94% FTP. Short accelerations use effort and full recovery without a sprint power target inferred from FTP. [Coggan power levels](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/power-training-levels/)
- Selected sessions use cycling-specific HR bounds only. A recorded threshold or maximum HR alone does not create zones. RPE covers riders without suitable data and short efforts where HR response lags. Running prescriptions retain their existing representation and rules.
- Activity IQ resamples timestamped records to one-second sample-and-hold sections across intervals up to three seconds. Explicit pause seconds are excluded and a missing sample or pause breaks the rolling window. Recorded zero watts count as coasting. Gaps and invalid values stay absent. NP requires at least five minutes, at least 90% active power coverage and at least 80% of possible complete 30-second windows. It takes each rolling mean to the fourth power, averages the results and takes the fourth root. IF equals NP divided by FTP effective on the ride date. Power-based stress uses confirmed moving seconds divided by 3,600, multiplied by IF squared and 100. Its duration must agree with the sampled active span within 5% or five seconds. VI equals NP divided by measured average power. [Coggan definitions](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/learn/articles/normalized-power-intensity-factor-training-stress/)
- Zone totals use covered seconds only. The recorded power chart leaves gaps. Decoupling compares mean watts per bpm in equal halves after removing five minutes from each end of a steady indoor ride of at least 30 minutes. It requires complete paired data across the compared span, VI no higher than 1.06 and power CV no higher than 12%. These quality gates are implementation heuristics. Heat, hydration and sensor placement can change the comparison.
- A timed comparison requires a unique existing activity link and intact time-based canonical steps. It compares totals. Interval execution needs verified provider step alignment. Recent minutes are recorded history and can be incomplete. Indoor classification uses explicit subtype information. Outdoor classification needs recorded GPS evidence.

## Adaptation, ownership and provider boundaries

The cycling recommendation is an experimental, read-only option after repeated athlete-reported high fatigue. It names the next eligible demanding ride and shows a shorter easy alternative. It cannot write a revision, reclaim missed minutes, edit completed rides, change FTP or publish. Positive execution never triggers an increase. Further cycling adjustment and match work requires a sport-specific adapter for the existing server evidence, consent, revision and ownership safeguards. The Running RPC and registry remain unchanged.

Cycling profile and workouts stay in the existing private authenticated training document. The saved profile accepts explicitly sourced validated FTP observations. No Garmin FTP feed has been verified. Build identifies recorded riding history, confirmation inputs and unavailable fitness values. Analysis reads detailed samples only for an owned activity through the existing authenticated reader. No public or social render receives private cycling metrics.

The SDK-verified local FIT encoder covers time, distance, open/manual-lap transitions, repeats expanded to at most 50 steps, absolute watts, absolute bpm, and RPE as notes. The verified Running Training API mapper and publication eligibility rules remain Running-only. A cycling Training API payload, identity/revision binding, retry deduplication, actual step matching and provider readback require isolated sandbox evidence before enabling any cycling publication. [Garmin FIT workout encoding](https://developer.garmin.com/fit/cookbook/encoding-workout-files/) · [Garmin Training API](https://developer.garmin.com/gc-developer-program/enterprise/training-api/)

## Release review

The 34-profile fixture and 1,088 scenario audit exercise arithmetic and scheduling invariants. They are synthetic engineering checks. Independent qualified cycling review still needs to examine interval doses, taper choices, short preparation and long rides, novice progression, threshold assessment, effort bands, multisport load and the repeated-fatigue suggestion. Validate the sampling and missing-data choices against representative files and confirm the coaching boundaries with reviewers before a hosted athlete beta. Hosted private accounts, device rendering and actual Garmin API or physical device results are separate checks.
