# Batch 92 Apple Health and Apple Watch

The draft builds on Swimming PR #145 at `938f75abded07e2bf22a8cf0d5ec4fa0f492a767`. Production and previous PR branches remain unchanged.

## Beta implementation

- The native staging build reuses MILE authentication, Calendar and the canonical running workout. Apple workout summaries are stored separately from the generic training document. Private Dashboard and Running MILE IQ read those records through the signed-in owner. They never enter the backup, social auto-sharing or image sharing paths. Older web and Garmin paths remain available.
- HealthKit first requests workouts only. A separate action requests optional heart rate, routes, running dynamics, resting HR, HRV (SDNN), sleep and VO₂ max. The only optional metric uploaded is average workout HR. Detailed routes, lap times, running dynamics and recovery observations stay in memory on the device. Missing samples stay unavailable. Apple measurements never fill Garmin recovery fields. Lap times are recorded events and supply no inferred distances. [HealthKit privacy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/protecting-user-privacy) · [App Review Guidelines §5.1.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#health-and-health-research)
- A stable UTC windowing scheme and secure cursor per MILE account, installation and consent grant support incremental and historical imports. The first window covers at least 180 days. UTC is used for the workout's calendar day only when HealthKit supplies no source time zone. Two members on one phone cannot silently share a Health store. Disconnect purges imported rows; it does not revoke Apple's system permissions or delete Apple Health records. A completed Calendar session can be unlinked if its source was deleted.
- Background delivery marks that HealthKit changed. An opted-in, still-authenticated foreground session checks for changes via the observer and native lifecycle. There is no background network upload while suspended. Loss of authorization or empty results does not delete data; only explicit HealthKit deletion records or explicit disconnect do. The per-account consent epoch and deletion ledger reject stale requests and deleted-ID replay. Both known-source and ambiguous overlaps with Garmin require individual review; uncertain Apple rows are held out of totals until reviewed. [Background delivery entitlement](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery)
- WorkoutKit maps canonical outdoor run steps to distance/time goals, work/recovery intervals, and supported pace/HR alerts. Repeats are expanded without changing their sequence. HR-triggered endings and other unsupported goals are withheld. Each schedule needs a deliberate confirmation and system authorization. A stable ID avoids rescheduling a revised workout automatically. The phone verifies the scheduler list; actual watch receipt and execution are separate. [WorkoutKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/workoutkit)
- Shared cloud sync, workout revision and Garmin publication gates are unchanged. Apple data is not used for advertising, unrelated analytics, iCloud or physiological claims. This patch does not replace the legacy web auth storage used by the shared Capacitor shell. A complete native session-vault cutover and signed-device account-switch test remain release gates.

## Exact preparation sequence

1. **Owner on iPhone or web** — Enable two-factor authentication on the Apple Account that will own the release. Choose the legal entity and App Store seller identity. For organisation enrolment, prepare the D‑U‑N‑S number, authority and contact information Apple asks for. Use the Apple Developer app or enrolment site. The owner must authorise any membership expense. [Apple enrolment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)
2. **Owner in Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles / App Store Connect** — Register the final explicit bundle ID; enable HealthKit and HealthKit Background Delivery; create the iOS app record on the same team. Give a builder the minimum required team permissions. Keep signing certificates, provisioning profiles, private keys, and account credentials outside the repository and chat.
3. **Staging only** — The read-only catalog check found the Running match RPC installed and the Apple consent/workout tables and page RPC absent. Inspect the migration ledger again before making changes. If not yet present, apply the reviewed Batch 86 device schema from `supabase/proposals/batch86-device-sync.sql` before `supabase/migrations/20261128113000_apple_health_beta.sql`. The latter also requires the existing Running manual-match schema. Provision verified synthetic MILE members. Confirm the iOS WebView origin can reach staging Auth and REST, and validate sign-up, recovery links, consent, staged Garmin and logout. The Apple-enabled build replaces production Supabase references and gates calls to MILE Staging. Physical Garmin Connect authorisation that requires a web preview should still be performed from the existing protected staging web origin.
4. **Builder on a Mac with Xcode 26** — Check out the reviewed commit. From `native/`, run `npm ci` then `MILE_NATIVE_STAGING_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=<staging-public-key> MILE_IOS_BUNDLE_ID=<registered-id> npm run prepare:ios`. Open `ios/App/App.xcodeproj`. Select the registered team under Signing & Capabilities and verify both HealthKit entitlements, usage descriptions and the bundled privacy manifest. Choose a unique increasing build number and verify the Archive build settings. Generated project files remain untracked.
5. **Owner and tester on paired iPhone and Apple Watch** — Direct-install a signed development build first. Test allowing and denying optional categories, one workout import/reimport/deletion, disconnect, switching MILE accounts, and reopening after a background change. Check local routes and timezone around midnight. Test one short safe canonical run with a time step and a distance step, verify the watch's displayed targets and transitions, record a completed workout, then confirm its Calendar match. Also test one Garmin overlap. Leave HR-triggered step endings and automatic scheduling or republication disabled.
6. **Builder and owner in App Store Connect** — After a physical smoke pass, Archive and Validate in Xcode Organizer and upload the reviewed build. Review the health-data privacy labels and policy, data deletion, proof of consent, export compliance, tester instructions, regional availability, and the complete privacy manifest for the app and dependencies. Keep health data out of screenshot examples and tester notes. This implementation only adds Batch 92 manifest declarations; the final App Privacy submission must cover the entire app. [Apple privacy manifest](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy-manifest-files)
7. **Owner** — Use an internal TestFlight group only for App Store Connect users who already need access. For the 10–15 runners, create a private external group, submit the first build for TestFlight review, and invite by email after approval. Verify the first two synthetic/tester accounts before expanding. Keep device observations and routes out of any feedback attachments. Disable the beta build or revoke the invitations to pause distribution without altering production. [TestFlight overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview/)

## What automation can prove

Native JavaScript and PostgreSQL tests cover payload isolation, consent, pagination, idempotency, deletion and matching. Browser fixtures exercise the actual Settings and Running Analysis flows with synthetic native responses. Unsigned native builds validate compilation and bundle structure. They cannot establish Apple Health access, background wake-up, HealthKit queries returning live data, watch receipt, physical timings, signed entitlements or TestFlight approval.
