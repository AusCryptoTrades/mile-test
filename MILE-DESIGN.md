# MILE design

Use the app’s monochrome surfaces and existing theme tokens. Follow the current headings, compact cards and in-app navigation. Maintain readable text, visible keyboard focus and touch targets of at least 44 px where practical. Use the approved onboarding design for account and athlete setup.

Plan review and Calendar should show the same structured steps, volume or time, estimated duration, target, purpose and focus. Mark estimates and unavailable measurements explicitly. Keep Garmin matches as candidates until their identity is confirmed. Private Analysis can show evidence and before-and-after proposals. Only the explicit approval action can change a prescription.

Check iPhone-sized layouts, dark and light appearance, loading, empty, connection errors and scrolling. Keep optional screenshots focused on changed screens and use synthetic athletes.
