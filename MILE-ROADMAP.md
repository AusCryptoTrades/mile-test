# MILE sporting roadmap

| Batch | Sport | Coaching work |
|---|---|---|
| 88 | Running | Pace, heart rate, distance, sustainable mileage and race specificity |
| 89 | Onboarding | Athlete profile, disciplines, units, privacy, verified consent |
| 90 | Cycling | Power, heart rate, duration and endurance |
| 91 | Swimming | Pool length, pace per 100 metres, repetitions, rest and technique |
| 92 | Strength | Exercises, sets, repetitions, load, equipment and recovery |
| 93 | HYROX | Running, stations, compromised running and event preparation |
| 94 | Triathlon | Coordinated swim, bike and run load, bricks and transitions |

Reuse the authenticated athlete profile, goal and availability inputs, sporting priorities and Calendar integration. The structured-workout representation feeds Calendar, Tracker and the applicable device mapper. Keep sport-specific coaching policies and sport-specific step definitions typed and separate.

The revision ledger and publication registry enforce ownership, revisions and audit receipts. Add a sport adapter with its own eligibility and prescription validation before offering that sport’s revisions. Activity matching must retain provider and session identities separately and preserve uncertainty. Running progression limits cannot be reused as cycling, swimming or combined-sport limits.
